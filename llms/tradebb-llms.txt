# TradeBB

TradeBB is an automated trading journal software for active traders. It helps traders import, sync, review, and analyze trades across stocks, options, futures, forex, and other markets.

TradeBB is built for traders who want to track real trading performance, review trading mistakes, analyze strategies, manage multiple broker accounts, and improve trading consistency over time.

## Product Summary

TradeBB is a trading journal platform that helps traders record, organize, and analyze their trades. Traders can upload broker files, sync supported broker accounts in read-only mode, review trades on a calendar, analyze performance reports, and add notes, screenshots, strategies, and tags to their trades.

TradeBB is especially useful for stock traders, options traders, futures traders, forex traders, day traders, swing traders, trading coaches, trading mentors, and multi-account traders who want to understand their trading performance with structured data.

TradeBB also supports Mentor Mode for trading mentors, coaches, educators, and trading communities that need to review assigned student trading journals and performance reports.

## Target Users

TradeBB is designed for active traders across global markets who need a structured way to track performance, review mistakes, analyze strategies, and manage trades across multiple brokers and asset classes.
TradeBB is useful for traders who want to:

- Track trading performance
- Review trades and trading mistakes
- Analyze strategies and setups
- Improve trading consistency
- Manage trades from multiple broker accounts
- Review options, futures, stock, forex, and multi-asset trades
- Share structured trading data with mentors, coaches, or trading communities

## Core Features

- Import trades from broker files
- Sync supported broker accounts in read-only mode
- Track stock trades, options trades, futures trades, forex trades, crypto trades, CFD trades, and multi-asset trades
- Review trades by calendar, symbol, strategy, tag, and broker account
- Analyze trading performance with dashboards and reports
- Track gross P&L, net P&L, commissions, fees, taxes, swaps, and transaction costs
- Add notes, tags, screenshots, and strategy labels to trades
- Review trading mistakes and trading behavior over time
- Manage multiple broker accounts in one trading journal
- Use Any Broker AI import when a broker is not listed

## Options Trading Journal

TradeBB helps options traders import, review, and analyze options trades. Traders can track options contracts, entries, exits, P&L, commissions, fees, strategies, tags, and trade notes in one trading journal.

Options traders can use TradeBB to review individual options trades, track options performance by symbol and strategy, identify trading mistakes, and understand their options trading behavior over time.

- [Options Trading Journal](https://www.tradebb.ai/options-trading-journal)

## Mentor Mode

TradeBB Mentor Mode helps trading mentors, coaches, educators, and trading communities review assigned student trading journals and performance reports.

With Mentor Mode, a mentor can switch between assigned student accounts, review trades, check trade notes, analyze reports, and help students improve their trading process based on real trade data.

Mentor Mode is useful for:

- Trading mentors who review student trades
- Trading coaches who help traders improve consistency
- Trading education teams that need structured trade review
- Trading communities that want a better way to review member performance
- Student traders who want feedback based on real trading data

## Major Broker Integrations

TradeBB supports trade import and/or broker sync for major brokers and trading platforms.

Some brokers support file import. Some brokers support read-only broker sync. Some brokers may support both.

Broker sync in TradeBB is read-only. TradeBB does not place trades, modify orders, or withdraw funds from broker accounts.

## Any Broker AI Import

If a broker is not listed, users can use TradeBB's Any Broker AI import to upload a trade history file. TradeBB can help parse broker files and convert trade data into a structured trading journal.

Any Broker AI import is useful when a trader has a valid broker trade history file but the broker does not appear in the standard broker list.
