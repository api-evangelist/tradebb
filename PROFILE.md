# TradeBB

An automated, AI-assisted trading journal and trade-analysis tool that imports, syncs, reviews, and analyzes trades across stocks, options, futures, forex, crypto, CFD, and multi-asset markets. Offers broker-file import, read-only broker sync, performance dashboards, and a Mentor Mode for coaches and educators.
